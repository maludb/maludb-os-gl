# Writing to customers

- **Plain and short.** The number, the amount, the due date, how to pay, the secure link. One sentence of thanks.
- **A reminder escalates by days past due, never by tone**: a first reminder assumes the invoice was missed; a second asks for
  a date; a third says what happens next (a statement, a call from a person). Never threaten; never mention collections or
  legal steps — a person decides those.
- **The secure link is the invoice.** It shows the document, what was received, what is owed, and lets the customer say
  "I paid this on … by …". Say that in every email; never paste the token anywhere else.
- **Never**: another customer's name or balance; an internal note; the bookkeeper's doubts; a bank account number beyond what
  the business's settings put on the invoice; a figure the tools did not give.
- **Statements** list the opening balance, each invoice, receipt and credit in date order and the running balance, for the
  period asked, in the customer's currency.
